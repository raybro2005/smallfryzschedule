Reading the calendar

What a day can be

12Stream. He went live, or a stream is on the books. Past streams fade a little so upcoming ones stand out.

12No stream. Nothing went out that day. Back-to-back days off join into one long blob.

12Event. Something that runs across days, like a voting window or a viewer tourney. It sits under the numbers and can share a day with a stream.

Little markers

  • ✓ It happened as planned.
  • ? Announced but not confirmed yet, or no start time given.
  • Live at the last update.
  • 🎃 Holidays and special days get a sticker.
  • Faded numbers with no blob are days this page has no record for yet.

Times

The whole calendar follows your own time zone: each stream sits on the day it starts where you are, and its start time shows in your time with UTC beside it. Flip the switch under the calendar to see everything in UTC instead.

He usually goes live around 20:00–21:00 UTC (that's afternoon in North America and late evening in Europe) and streams four to six hours. Click any day for the title, collab partners and peak viewers.
